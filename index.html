Privacy Policy — Machebhate-A (মাছেভাতে-অ)

Developed by NEXT IN GLOBAL | Last Updated: October 2026

Welcome to Machebhate-A (মাছেভাতে-অ) ("App"), a specialized food ordering and delivery platform developed and operated by NEXT IN GLOBAL ("we," "our," or "us"). We are deeply committed to protecting your personal information, safeguarding your privacy, and complying with Google Play Store data safety standards. If you have any questions or concerns about this privacy policy, please contact us at Machebhate@gmail.com.

1. Information We Collect

To provide a smooth food delivery service, we collect the following categories of information when you use our app:

  • Personal Identification & Phone Number: When you register or log in, we collect your mobile number to create your unique user account and deliver One-Time Passwords (OTPs) for secure authentication via Firebase.
  • Profile Information: Optional details such as your display name and email address provided during profile setup.
  • Precise Location Data: With your explicit permission, we collect approximate or precise GPS location data to auto-detect delivery addresses, calculate delivery distances, and connect you with nearby food partners.
  • Delivery Addresses & Instructions: Saved delivery addresses, apartment/house numbers, landmarks, and specific delivery instructions provided by you.
  • Cart, Wishlist & Order History: Details of food items added to your cart, active food orders, past order history, meal preferences, and transaction records.
  • Payment & Financial Information: Order payment status, transaction reference IDs, and payment method details processed securely through certified third-party payment gateways (UPI, Credit/Debit cards, Net Banking). Note: We do not store complete credit card numbers or UPI PINs on our servers.
  • Device & Technical Diagnostics: Device identifiers, operating system version, app version, IP address, crash logs, and performance diagnostics to maintain application stability and security.
  • Push Notification Tokens: Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) tokens to send real-time order status updates, kitchen dispatch alerts, and delivery notifications.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information strictly for operating and enhancing our food delivery service, including:

  • Creating and managing your account and authenticating logins securely via OTP.
  • Processing your food orders, coordinating with partner kitchens, and assigning delivery personnel.
  • Sharing necessary delivery details (Name, Phone Number, Delivery Address) with delivery partners to ensure successful food drop-offs.
  • Sending critical transactional notifications regarding order preparation, dispatch, and delivery updates.
  • Handling online payments, issuing applicable refunds, and resolving billing inquiries.
  • Improving user interface design, app performance, localized food recommendations, and customer support.

3. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We respect your privacy and do not sell your personal data. We share information only with trusted entities necessary for service fulfillment:

  • Delivery Personnel & Kitchen Partners: Necessary order and contact details are shared with assigned delivery agents and kitchens solely to fulfill your food order.
  • Secure Payment Gateways: Certified payment processors to securely handle online transactions.
  • Infrastructure & Cloud Services: Google Firebase services used for secure backend database management, authentication, and push notifications.
  • Legal Compliance: When required by law, court orders, or governmental regulations to protect our legal rights or user safety.

4. Data Security & Retention

We implement robust technical and organizational security measures, including HTTPS data encryption, secure Firebase Firestore database security rules, and token-based authentication. We retain your personal data only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy or to comply with legal and tax obligations.

5. Your Privacy Rights & Account Deletion

You have full control over your personal data. You can access, update, or correct your profile information directly within the app settings. You also have the absolute right to request permanent account and data deletion at any time by visiting our dedicated Account Deletion Page or by emailing us at Machebhate@gmail.com.

6. Children's Privacy

Machebhate-A is not directed at children under the age of 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from minors.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect operational or legal changes. Any updates will be posted on this page with the revised date.

Refund & Cancellation Policy — Machebhate-A

Developed by NEXT IN GLOBAL | Last Updated: October 2026

At Machebhate-A (মাছেভাতে-অ) (developed by NEXT IN GLOBAL), we strive to ensure high-quality food preparation and reliable delivery. Please review our cancellation and refund guidelines below.

1. Order Cancellation Rules

Users may request cancellation of an order directly through the app. However, cancellation eligibility depends strictly on the order status:

  • Before Kitchen Acceptance: Orders can typically be cancelled without penalty before the kitchen accepts and starts preparing the food.
  • After Preparation Begins: Once food preparation has commenced or the order has been dispatched, cancellations are generally not permitted.

2. Prepaid Refunds

If an eligible order paid via online prepayment is successfully cancelled or declined due to item unavailability:

  • Approved refunds will be processed back to the original payment method.
  • Depending on your bank or payment gateway, processing times typically take between 5 to 7 business days.
